01 / THE APPROACH

From examples to executable formulas.

Rather than hand-writing a solver for every financial template, the system searches for compact expressions that reproduce labeled outputs and intermediate reasoning steps.

01

Fingerprint

Mask numeric values and retain trace-slot structure to identify a template.

02

Extract inputs

Parse numeric tokens into exact rational values.

03

Synthesize

Search an arithmetic grammar, pruning candidates by their values over examples.

04

Chain steps

Reuse learned intermediate expressions as building blocks.

05

Verify or abstain

Evaluate exactly, format to the specification, validate, or abstain.

Why exact arithmetic?

Financial answers can fail because of rounding conventions. The system checks candidate calculations with rational arithmetic and applies the answer's declared formatting rules.

02 / EVALUATION

Strong precision. Limited coverage.

The paper reports a conservative learner. It answers when it finds a formula consistent with its examples and otherwise abstains.

03 / LIMITATIONS

Verification is not generalization.

Exact arithmetic confirms that a recovered expression reproduces the provided examples. It does not prove that the expression captures the intended financial rule on unseen inputs.

Template coverage

Long numeric lists, deeper formulas, and enumerated answers limited coverage even among templates represented in released examples.

Hidden templates

A learner relying on examples from known templates cannot automatically recover formulas for templates with no released examples.

Reproducibility

Archived scripts use machine-specific paths and organizer modules. Environment and path cleanup is still required.

Research integrity note

The official result was rank 27 of 27. This presentation includes that result rather than presenting held-out precision as the overall competition outcome.
